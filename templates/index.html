Paste rows from Excel with a header row. Recognized columns:
Name, Address, City, State, Zip, Type, SF, Lat, Lon, Units, Year Built, Notes
Lat and Lon columns are required for plotting on the map.
Tip: Lat is positive in the Northern Hemisphere (e.g. US, Europe) and negative in the Southern (e.g. Australia). Lon is negative in the Western Hemisphere (e.g. Americas) and positive in the Eastern (e.g. Europe, Asia).
